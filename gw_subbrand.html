Sam's Club (Walmart): rebrand launched with Lebanon, TN — new logo, format, “Come Join Us” slogan; FY26 comp (incl. fuel) +2.9%. Katy TX (Texas Heritage Marketplace, NewQuest $400M mixed-use) confirmed as 7th FY26 site.
Harris Teeter (Kroger): clear growth outlier — 5-site pipeline in the Southeast (Jacksonville FL, Clemson SC, Kannapolis NC, Lake Wylie SC, Fort Mill SC), even as some Harris Teeter locations appear on Kroger’s closure-tracker lists.
Mariano's (Kroger): Buffalo Grove IL confirmed closed 8/8; Bloomingdale IL (8/15) and Glenview West/Northbrook IL (8/22) close this month — accelerating the IL banner drawdown under Kroger’s 60-store closure program.
GreenWise Market (Publix): the standalone banner was discontinued in 2023; survives only as a private-label product brand — its frozen berries were recalled for E. coli in July 2026.
Amazon Fresh / Amazon Go: both branded physical chains remain fully wound down (closed Jan–Mar 2026); no relaunch confirmed this cycle. Some sites are converting to Whole Foods or the new Daily Shop small-urban format — now 8 open (NYC metro, Arlington VA, London); 5 additional Daily Shop locations confirmed by end of 2026, with leases signed in Boston, Chicago, and Philadelphia.
