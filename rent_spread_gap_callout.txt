Rent escalator / step disclosure remains thin industry-wide. KRG and PECO are the only names in the peer set that consistently disclose a clean contractual annual step rate in public filings (~2% and ~2% respectively). All other peers (REG, FRT, BRX, KIM, UE, IVT, ADC, AKR, CURB) do not provide this disclosure at a portfolio level. This is a known industry-wide limitation and does not reflect a research gap — the data does not exist in public disclosure for most names.
