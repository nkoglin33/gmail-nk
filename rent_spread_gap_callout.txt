Rent-step/escalator disclosure remains limited industry-wide. Of this peer set, KRG and PECO are the most consistent disclosers of contractual annual escalators (KRG: ~1.5–2.0% annually; PECO: embedded escalators noted in supplementals). BRX, KIM, FRT, IVT, and AKR do not provide a clean escalator figure in their standard investor materials. REG does not disclose this separately. This is a known, persistent industry-wide disclosure gap — not a research failure — and limits apples-to-apples rent-step comparisons across the peer set.
