Rent-step / escalator disclosure remains thin industry-wide. Among this peer set, only KRG and PECO publish a clean annual contractual escalator figure (KRG: ~1.5–2.0% weighted average; PECO: ~1.9–2.0% per supplemental). The other peers do not separately disclose contractual bump schedules. This is an industry-wide disclosure gap, not a research failure — the absence of a number is itself meaningful context for renewal-spread analysis.
