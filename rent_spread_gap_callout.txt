Rent-step / escalator disclosure: Industry-wide disclosure of contractual annual rent increases remains thin. Among this peer set, KRG and PECO are the only companies that consistently provide a clean contractual escalator figure (~1.5–2.0% for KRG; PECO has disclosed escalators in supplemental materials). Most peers (REG, BRX, KIM, FRT, IVT, UE, AKR, CURB) do not separately quantify contractual step-ups in public filings. This is a known industry-wide disclosure limitation — not a data gap specific to this report. Where escalator data is not disclosed, the absence of a figure is expected and not a data quality flag.
