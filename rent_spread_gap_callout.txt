Rent-step / contractual escalator disclosure remains a known industry-wide gap. Of the 9-peer direct comp set, only KRG (~1.5–2.0% annual contractual escalators) and PECO provide a clean escalator disclosure in their quarterly supplementals. REG, BRX, KIM, FRT, AKR, UE, and CURB do not separately quantify contractual rent steps in public filings. This is not a research failure — it reflects standard REIT disclosure practice; most peers embed escalator economics in blended cash spread figures rather than breaking them out separately. Tracking this gap each quarter is the primary purpose of this row.
