• Pre-earnings positioning dominates; grocery-anchored earns maximum analyst conviction. With the entire retail REIT universe reporting Q2 within seven days of July 25 (AKR July 28, REG/BNL July 29, KIM/KRG/ADC July 30, IVT Aug 3, MAC/SKT Aug 4), this week was defined by analyst actions rather than earnings moves. REG received simultaneous price-target increases from Barclays ($90, Overweight), Raymond James ($88, Outperform), Citi ($82, Neutral), and Evercore ($80, In Line); KRG's Piper Sandler target jumped from $27 to $33; KIM was upgraded at Wells Fargo — all pre-print. Regency read-through: the breadth of upward analyst conviction entering Q2 (Q1 SS NOI +4.4%, near-record occupancy ~97%) sets a high bar that management guided can be met in H2 after a modest Q2 below the +3.25%–3.75% full-year range.
  • EPRT and Getty beat Q2; net lease bifurcates on relative value. Essential Properties (EPRT) delivered Q2 AFFO of $0.50/share (+9% YoY) on July 22 with revenue +18.1%, raising investment guidance to $1.2–$1.5B and prompting a Stifel $36.25 price target — despite a slight post-report pullback on profit-taking. Getty Realty (GTY) posted Q2 AFFO of $0.62/share (+5.1%) and raised 2026 AFFO guidance for the second consecutive time, with Q2 acquisitions at a 7.4% initial cash yield. NNN, by contrast, has underperformed retail REIT peers YTD despite a 37th consecutive annual dividend increase (+3.3%), with sell-side noting "fully priced" near-term outlook.
  • Mall/lifestyle hits multi-year highs; SPG leads on Goldman Buy + Piper $285 target. Simon Property Group surged +2.1% on July 24 alone after Goldman Sachs initiated at Buy and Piper Sandler raised its target to $285 (from $230), reflecting Q1 domestic NOI growth of +6.7% and mall occupancy of 99.2% (+80 bps). Macerich (MAC) set a 52-week high of $25.97 on July 17, returning +42% YTD, while Acadia (AKR) and Tanger (SKT) approached Q2 reports near 52-week highs. The segment remains bifurcated: CBL's trailing 52-week return of +90% contrasts with PREIT's cautionary OTC illiquidity after its second bankruptcy exit in April 2024.
  • WSR delisted; CURB near all-time high — public strip universe narrows further. Whitestone REIT (WSR) completed its $1.7B Ares Real Estate acquisition at $19.00/share, with NYSE listing formally ended July 14 — the third retail REIT absorbed by private capital since early 2023 (also ROIC/Blackstone Feb 2025, STOR/GIC Feb 2023). Curbline Properties (CURB) set a new all-time high of $31.89 with Q1 same-property NOI of +4.8%, supported by a $308M June 2026 forward equity offering to fund a $700M single-asset acquisition pipeline. With ROIC in Blackstone's hands and STOR fully private, the active public grocery-anchored/strip universe is concentrated in four names: REG, KIM, KRG, IVT.
  • Public-private spread persists at 145 bps; institutional capital floods grocery-anchored. REIT-implied cap rates (~5.9–6.2%) remain ~145 bps wide to private appraisals (~4.4%), a record 17 consecutive quarters of divergence. Grocery-anchored transaction volume reached $12.8B in the rolling four quarters through Q1 2026 (+42% YoY), with institutional buyers at a 10+ year high share of 27%. JLL pegs national grocery-anchored cap rates at ~6.7%, compressing ~40 bps from the cycle peak; top-tier anchors (Publix, Trader Joe's) trade at 5.5%. Regency read-through: the portfolio's institutional-quality anchor base and infill locations position it at the tightest end of that range, supporting premium valuation and NAV expansion relative to peers.
