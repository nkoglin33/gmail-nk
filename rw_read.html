• REITs Close September Mixed, Rate Sensitivity in Focus. Retail REITs drifted slightly negative at the open of September 29 (retail sector -0.33%) as investors positioned ahead of Q3 earnings season. Grocery-anchored names held up better than malls and net lease, consistent with the fundamental divergence theme that has defined 2026. Regency's forward earnings calendar is now firmed: Q3 results on November 5 after market close.
  • Institutional Capital Piling Into Grocery-Anchored. Cap rates for top-market grocery-anchored centers (Publix/Trader Joe's-anchored) have compressed to 5.25%–5.8%, with the national average at 6.7% — decade-low levels attracting institutional buyers competitively. Regency read-through: the portfolio trades well above replacement cost, development yields look increasingly attractive relative to the acquisition market, and the demand-supply imbalance continues to support rent growth.
  • Truist Initiates SPG at Hold, PT $215; Barclays Trims KIM to $27. Two notable sell-side moves this week. Truist's new Hold on Simon Property Group (PT $215, October 2) reflects concerns about the gap between Class A and secondary mall performance. Barclays maintained Overweight on KIM but cut its price target to $27, citing near-term leasing economics. REG consensus analyst target remains ~$86, implying ~17% upside from current levels.
  • Regency Pays $0.755 Quarterly Dividend October 2. The Q3 2026 common dividend ($0.755/share, declared August 5 with Q2 results) was paid October 2 — a raise from the prior $0.67/quarter. Preferred dividends (Series A $0.391; Series B $0.367) pay October 30. The raise reflects management confidence in sustained FFO growth at the high end of the $4.84–$4.88 guidance range.
  • REG Expands EV Charging Partnership with EVGO. Regency extended its EVGO fast-charging network across additional portfolio centers this week, positioning grocery-anchored locations as daily-use infrastructure. Beyond an amenity, EV charging reinforces the traffic-generation story that underpins anchor rent renewal leverage — particularly relevant as REG approaches Q3 lease-up with record occupancy to defend.
