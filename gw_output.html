This Week's Read
  • Kroger's August closure wave hits Mariano's hardest. Three suburban Chicago Mariano's locations close this month — Buffalo Grove (8/8), Bloomingdale (8/15), Glenview West (8/22) — plus Alpharetta GA (8/16), Charlottesville VA (8/22), and Gassaway WV (8/22). Six confirmed August closures represent one of the most concentrated monthly waves yet in Kroger's 60-store program. Kroger's stock is down ~26% from its March high.
  • Publix deepens its Carolina footprint with Mills River, NC opening (8/12). The 50,325-sq-ft Marketplace at Mills River store opens Wednesday — Publix's push into western NC near Hendersonville/Asheville. Three more FL openings follow immediately (Ocala 8/10, Coral Springs 8/17, Winter Garden 8/17), on top of Ruskin/Apollo Beach on 8/13. Despite four straight quarters of negative/slowing comps, Publix is spending $2.4B+ in FY26 capex on expansion and redevelopment.
  • Trader Joe's Mandeville, LA opened August 5. First north-shore New Orleans location (Pontchartrain Square, US Hwy 190) — part of a 25+ site 2026 national expansion. Store count now approximately 662 US stores in 42+ states. TJ's pace of ~one new store per week continues with no signs of slowing.
  • Whole Foods' next US opening is two weeks away. Montgomery Township (Skillman), NJ opens August 21, 2026 — a 45,000-sq-ft format store anchoring a Princeton-area trade area. WFM continues at roughly one new US full-size store every 2–4 weeks toward its 100+ new store target.
  • Sam's Club confirms Katy, TX as 7th FY26 site — a $400M mixed-use anchor. The Katy location will anchor NewQuest Properties' Texas Heritage Marketplace, a $400M mixed-use development on the Katy Freeway. Alongside Baytown TX and Lathrop CA, this is the third confirmed Sam's Club FY26 opening embedded in a shopping-center or mixed-use project — directly relevant to net-lease landlords.
  • Wegmans' Pittsburgh groundbreaking is next week (8/13); Charlotte NC on track for October 14. Cranberry Township, PA breaks ground Thursday — first western PA store, opens fall 2027. Charlotte's Ballantyne campus store (11550 N Community House Rd, ~110,000 sq ft, ~450 jobs) remains the near-term headline, opening 10/14/26 as Wegmans' first NC store.
