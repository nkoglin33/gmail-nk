• Kroger acquires Giant Eagle for $1.65B — largest deal since failed Albertsons merger. Announced 7/1/26 (first captured this cycle): ~197 supermarkets + 11 pharmacies across OH, PA, WV, MD, and IN add ~$9B in annual sales. Close expected 2027 pending FTC; limited store divestitures anticipated in overlapping Ohio/PA markets. Q2 FY26 earnings call is this Thursday, 9/11.
  • Trader Joe's Merriam KS opened 9/2/26 — first grocery store in that city in 8 years. The 9,498 sq ft store at Grand Station Marketplace anchors a $102M mixed-use redevelopment of the former Antioch Library site; first Kansas City metro TJ's location, 50+ jobs. Hundreds lined up before sunrise on opening day.
  • Whole Foods opens back-to-back California stores this month: Elk Grove 9/16, downtown San Diego 9/23. Elk Grove (43K sq ft, 94th CA store) and San Diego (45,400 sq ft urban core, 95th CA store) complete a two-week California sprint. Both feature 1,500+ local items and Grocery Pickup.
  • Publix September cluster incoming: Elizabethtown KY (9/23) and Apopka FL (9/24). Elizabethtown (50,325 sq ft, Shops at Pear Orchard) is Publix's second KY market; Apopka (48K sq ft, Shoppes at East Shore) adds to dense Central FL footprint. Both open 16–17 days from now.
