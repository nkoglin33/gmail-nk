• Sam's Club exits a 7-year growth pause. Walmart's Sam's Club opened a rebranded Lebanon, TN club July 30 — new logo, format, and a stated long-term target of ~15 new clubs/year. Confirmed 2026 openings span TN, CA (x2), TX (x3); several anchor larger shopping-center/mixed-use redevelopments (Baytown TX, Lathrop CA) — directly relevant to net-lease/shopping-center landlords.
  • Kroger stock down ~26% from its March high. KR closed $56.68 on 8/5/26, down from a 52-week high of $76.58 hit 3/12/26. Q1 FY26 identical sales ex-fuel decelerated to +1.0% (from +3.2% a year ago) as the company runs an 18-month, 60-store closure program (through year-end 2026) alongside a $3.8–4.0B remodel-focused capex plan with no net-new stores company-wide.
  • Publix posts a 4th straight quarter of negative/slowing comps. Q2 2026 comp sales -0.5% (H1 -0.3%), which Publix ties to the Jan 2026 Medicare Maximum Fair Price drug-pricing change and consumer trade-down. Its internal employee stock price fell 4.1% to $19.60 (Aug 1 reset). Publix is still expanding — Kentucky (2nd store opened Cold Spring 6/17/26, 3 more pipelined) and continued Florida densification — alongside an active close/rebuild cycle on aging boxes.
  • Amazon leans further into Whole Foods + the Daily Shop format. Amazon's Physical Stores segment (WFM + remnants of Fresh/Go) grew net sales 3.6% YoY in Q2 2026 to $5.79B. CEO Andy Jassy said WFM growth is “significantly outpacing comparable grocers,” and that Daily Shop — the small urban format, now 8 locations — is “expanding very rapidly,” with signed leases in Boston, Chicago, and Philadelphia. Amazon Fresh and Amazon Go's branded physical stores are now fully wound down (closed Jan–Mar 2026); some sites are converting to WFM/Daily Shop.
  • Trader Joe's keeps a brisk, low-profile pace — and a growing labor story. 661 US stores as of 7/21/26 (net +5 in ~4 weeks); 25+ new stores planned across ~15 states in 2026. Separately, its Chicago (North Center) store became the 5th unionized Trader Joe's nationally July 8–9 — but none of the 5 unionized stores has a ratified contract more than 4 years after the first vote, and fresh NLRB unfair-labor-practice charges were filed 6/9/26.
  • Wegmans pushes south while its satisfaction scores slide. First Pittsburgh-area store breaks ground 8/13/26 (opens fall 2027); first North Carolina store (Charlotte/Ballantyne) opens 10/14/26. But Wegmans' ACSI score dropped sharply from 83 to 78 (2025→2026) — the steepest decline among major grocers measured — knocking it out of the top 10 supermarket ranking to 14th place.
