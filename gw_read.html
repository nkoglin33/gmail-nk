• Publix Florence KY opens as 10th Kentucky store; six more openings through Oct 30. Florence KY (Farmview Commons, 8470 US 42, 58,817 sq ft) opened Tuesday Oct 7 as the 3rd Northern Kentucky location and 10th statewide. Six more ribbon cuttings follow this month alone: Summerville SC & Hinesville GA (Oct 15), Fayetteville NC (Oct 21), Tampa FL (Oct 23), Calabash NC (Oct 28), Bainbridge GA (Oct 29), Palm Bay FL rebuild (Oct 30) — one of Publix’s most active single-month calendar stretches in 2026.
  • Trader Joe’s Herriman UT opened Oct 9 — Utah’s 8th store, first on Salt Lake County’s west side. The 9,200 sq ft store at The Commons at Herriman Towne Center (4918 W. 13400 S.) opened Friday at 9 a.m. with 60+ crew members hired locally. West Jordan UT is the next confirmed Utah pipeline site (no open date announced).
  • Wegmans Charlotte Ballantyne opens Wednesday Oct 14 — 2 days away. The 110,000 sq ft store at 11550 N Community House Rd (9 a.m.) is the 5th NC location and first Charlotte market entry, with 450 jobs, a French-inspired café, 400+ cheese varieties, and 286 indoor/outdoor seats. Pittsburgh Cranberry Township (fall 2027) is next.
  • Whole Foods announces 63,082 sq ft LA Bloom on Third flagship for Nov 11. The store at 355 S. Ogden Drive (Bloom on Third, at 3rd & Fairfax) is a relocation of the original Fairfax WFM (open since 2002), featuring a new taproom, charcuterie venue, and 1,900+ local California items. At 63K sq ft it is well above WFM’s ~40K sq ft average — announced via WFM press release Oct 8, 2026.
  • Kroger Owensboro corridor: East 4th St. closes by Nov 4; new Marketplace still no date. The replacement 100K sq ft, $35M Marketplace at 3914 Fairview Dr has no confirmed open date despite the existing East 4th St. store closing on or before 11/4/26. The site sits directly across from Publix Gateway Commons (opened 10/3) — making Owensboro one of the most contested mid-size grocery corridors in 2026 with a temporary anchor gap likely.
