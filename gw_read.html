• Publix Kentucky blitz: four stores in six weeks as Southern expansion hits full stride. Elizabethtown KY (9/23, 50,325 sq ft, The Shops at Pear Orchard) and Apopka FL (9/24, ~48K sq ft, The Shoppes at East Shore) open this week; Owensboro KY (Gateway Commons, 10/3) and Florence KY (Farmview Commons, 58,817 sq ft, 10/7) follow in the first week of October. Publix now has confirmed KY pipeline of 6+ locations with more undated.
  • Whole Foods Elk Grove CA confirmed open 9/16; downtown San Diego (9/23) opens in two days. Elk Grove (43K sq ft, 8100 Elk Grove Blvd, 94th CA store) opened as scheduled; San Diego (45,400 sq ft, 201 W. A Street, 95th CA store) opens Tuesday. Back-to-back California openings cement WFM as the most active national grocer this month — and push it to 100 CA stores within reach.
  • Trader Joe’s Lafayette LA grand opening 9/17: customers lining up before sunrise for first Acadiana location. The 9,000+ sq ft store at 1710 Camellia Blvd is TJ’s first in Lafayette and sixth in Louisiana. Merriam KS (9/2) and Lafayette LA (9/17) give TJ’s two September grand openings in markets previously underserved by the chain. Pipeline remains 25+ stores across 15+ states for 2026.
  • Kroger Flint Township MI closure now 9 days out (9/30/26) as year-end program deadline nears. Carman Plaza Kroger (G3288 Corunna Rd) closes September 30 — Michigan’s first confirmed closure under the 60-store program. Program includes closures across GA, IL, IN, KY, LA, MD, MI, NC, TN, TX, VA, WV, WI through year-end. Owensboro KY Marketplace replacement still targeted fall 2026 with no specific open date confirmed.
  • Wegmans Charlotte countdown: 23 days to Ballantyne grand opening (10/14/26). The 110,000 sq ft store at 11550 N Community House Rd (Ballantyne campus) will be Wegmans’ 5th NC location and first Charlotte market entry — 450 jobs, outdoor area with first-ever Wegmans field. Construction confirmed complete; hiring finalized.
  • Competitive flashpoint: Publix Owensboro KY and Kroger Marketplace Owensboro both opening early October — same corridor. Publix Gateway Commons opens 10/3; Kroger Marketplace (3914 Fairview Dr, 100K sq ft, $35M) targets fall 2026 — these two anchors land within weeks of each other on the same Owensboro commercial strip, with the old Kroger East 4th St. closing simultaneously. For shopping-center operators, both moves signal net new anchor demand in a mid-tier Kentucky market.
