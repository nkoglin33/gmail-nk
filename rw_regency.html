Regency Centers' Q3 2026 dividend ex-date of September 11, 2026 passed this week; shareholders of record September 12 will receive the $0.755/share quarterly dividend on October 1. Q2 2026 results (reported July 29) remain the most recent operational data point: 96.9% portfolio leased (+40bps YoY), blended cash rent spread of 10.4% (GAAP 19.5%), same-property NOI growth of +3.8%, and full-year NAREIT FFO guidance of $4.84–$4.88 per share (second upward revision of 2026). With Q3 ending September 30, Regency and its peers are entering their earnings quiet period; the market is now focused on whether the $41M signed-not-commenced pipeline — the largest in the company's disclosed history — converts at the pace implied by Q3 guidance, and on whether back-to-school traffic at grocery-anchored centers is tracking consistent with the positive tone management struck on the Q2 call. Q3 reporting is expected in late October 2026.
