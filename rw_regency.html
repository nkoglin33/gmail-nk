Regency Centers (REG) was priced at $71.60 as of October 2, 2026 (+3.72% YTD), ahead of its Q3 2026 earnings call scheduled November 5. Following the strong Q2 print on July 29 — 96.9% portfolio leased (+40bps YoY), 10.4% blended cash rent spread, SP NOI growth of +3.8%, and raised full-year FFO guidance of $4.84–$4.88 — Wells Fargo carries an Overweight with an $88 price target and UBS stands at $81. The Brixmor-Slate Grocery transaction (Sep 28, ~$636M for 23 centers at ~96% leased, FL/GA/Carolinas) crystallizes private-market pricing in REG's competitive tier: the implied cap rate near the ~6.7% national benchmark is directionally consistent with public-market valuations for REG's premium grocery-anchored portfolio, though REG's higher asset quality and major-market concentration should command a tighter cap-rate premium. The rising 10-year yield above 5% in early October is a macro headwind to watch heading into Q3 reporting, but REG's rent-escalator and re-leasing optionality offer more rate resilience than the long-duration net-lease peers hit hardest this week.
