Regency shares rose +0.9% on the week as the Q2 earnings cycle closed with all 9 direct competitors reporting — and every one raising 2026 guidance. REG's own Q2 print (reported July 29) remains the segment benchmark: Nareit FFO of $1.21/share (beat by $0.02), same-property NOI growth of +3.8%, portfolio 96.9% leased (+40 bps YoY), blended cash rent spread 10.4% (GAAP 19.5%), and full-year guidance raised for the second time to $4.84–$4.88 FFO / 3.7%–4.1% SP NOI growth. With peers now fully reported, the cross-peer comparison puts REG mid-pack on absolute occupancy (below PECO's record 97.3% and KIM's 96.4%), but distinguished by execution quality on the development and redevelopment program: the $680M in-process pipeline at ~9% blended return and ~80% pre-leased sits in front of ~$12.8B of trailing private-market grocery-anchored transaction demand clearing at 5.25–5.5% caps — meaningfully higher than the cost of creating product through development. The 240 bps leased-to-commenced gap and a $41M SNO pipeline (69% expected to commence by year-end) remain the primary 2027 NOI driver that peer comparisons do not yet capture. Raymond James ($88, Outperform) and BTIG ($85, Buy) raised price targets following Q2.
