Regency Centers (REG) heads into Q3 earnings season — expected around October 28, 2026 — with Q2 2026 fundamentals that positioned it near the top of the peer group: 96.9% leased (+40bps YoY), blended cash rent spread of 10.4% (GAAP 19.5%), same-property NOI growth of +3.8%, and FFO guidance of $4.84–$4.88. The team's focus entering Q4 is on the same-property NOI guidance band (3.7%–4.1%) and whether the hurricane-adjacent expense anomaly that weighed on some Q2 figures normalizes. With Q3 ends September 30, REG's next public update will frame the full-year picture in late October. In the interim, cap rate compression in grocery-anchored submarkets and continued leasing demand across REG's coastal and Sun Belt footprint represent the clearest near-term fundamental tailwinds for the platform.
