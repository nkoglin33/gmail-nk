Regency Centers (REG) reported Q2 2026 results on July 29, delivering Nareit FFO of $1.21/share vs. $1.19 consensus, same-property NOI growth of +3.8%, a portfolio-wide leased rate of 96.9% (+40 bps YoY), and blended cash rent spreads of 10.4% (GAAP 19.5%) — marking the second guidance raise of 2026, with full-year Nareit FFO now guided to $4.84–$4.88 and SP NOI growth to 3.7%–4.1%. The Q2 pace moderated slightly from Q1's +4.4% SP NOI (anticipated given a harder expense comparison), and management's guidance language signals re-acceleration in H2 as the signed-but-not-yet-open pipeline — representing a wide leased-to-occupied spread near cycle highs — begins to convert. The result was met positively by the sell side, with price target increases from Raymond James ($88, Outperform) and BTIG ($85, Buy), and reinforces REG's positioning as the grocery-anchored benchmark heading into UE's Q2 report (Aug 6) — the one remaining direct peer yet to report.
