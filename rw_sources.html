Methodology. This report covers 19 actively-traded public retail REITs organized into 4 segments: Convenience/Strip (1 name), Grocery-Anchored/Power (9), Mall/Lifestyle (5), Net Lease (4). Names that are no longer publicly traded or have been acquired (WSR, ROIC, UBA, PREIT, STOR, INRE) are excluded and do not appear as rows, even blank ones. Segments are fixed and were not modified from the prior cycle. This is a live web research pull — not a Bloomberg/FactSet-certified data extract — and should be spot-checked against certified sources before board-level or investment-committee use. Operating detail for all 9 direct REG peers is sourced from their most recent quarterly earnings releases; where a new release was not available this cycle, the prior figure is carried forward and labeled by quarter.
