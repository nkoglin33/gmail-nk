Methodology. This report covers 19 actively-traded public retail REITs organized into 4 segments: Convenience/Strip (1 name), Grocery-Anchored/Power (9), Mall/Lifestyle (5), Net Lease (4). Names that are no longer publicly traded or have been acquired (WSR, ROIC, UBA, PREIT, STOR, INRE) are excluded and do not appear as rows, even blank ones. Segments are fixed and were not modified from the prior cycle. This is a live web research pull — not a Bloomberg/FactSet-certified data extract — and should be spot-checked against certified sources before board-level or investment-committee use. Operating detail for all 9 direct REG peers is sourced from their most recent quarterly earnings releases; where a new release was not available since last week, the prior figure is carried forward and labeled by quarter.
Price data. Yahoo Finance chart API (https://query1.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart/{TICKER}) was again unavailable this cycle — the environment proxy returned policy-denial 403 on all attempted tickers. Price data was sourced via two methods: (1) a reference briefing document prepared internally for the week of August 10, 2026, which provided confirmed 5-day and year-to-date percentage returns for 16 of 19 tickers (excluding CBL, BNL, GTY); (2) web search against financial news sources (StockTitan, GlobeNewswire, MarketBeat, earnings press releases) for CBL, BNL, GTY and for absolute price levels. Absolute prices in the heatmap are estimated by applying confirmed or estimated 5D returns to last cycle's price estimates and are labeled accordingly. 5D return = close approximately August 7, 2026 vs. close approximately August 1, 2026 (5 trading sessions prior). YTD = approximately August 7 vs. December 31, 2025 close. 1-Yr = approximately August 7, 2026 vs. approximately August 7–8, 2025. YTD and 1-Yr percentage returns sourced from the internal briefing reference document or estimated from web search references; raw closing prices for December 31, 2025 and August 2025 were not directly confirmed this cycle. CURB 1-year return note: CURB spun off from SITE Centers in October 2024; any 1-year figure reflects a partial-year comparison only.
