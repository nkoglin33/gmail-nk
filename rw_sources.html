Methodology
This report is produced by a scheduled research agent using live web research. It is NOT a Bloomberg- or FactSet-certified data pull. All figures should be independently spot-checked before use in board-level, investor relations, or compliance-sensitive materials. Price data is machine-sourced from the Yahoo Finance chart API; operating metrics are sourced from company press releases and supplemental filings. The report is intended as a weekly monitoring tool for internal tracking purposes.

Price data
Price, 5-Day, YTD, and 1-Year returns for all 19 issuers sourced from the Yahoo Finance chart API v8 via the GitHub Actions scheduled snapshot workflow (reit-prices.yml). Closes as of Friday October 16, 2026. Methodology: Price = close on as_of date; 5D = vs. close one week earlier (October 9, 2026); YTD = vs. prior-year final close (December 31, 2025); 1-Yr = vs. close approximately 365 days earlier (October 16, 2025). One consistent as-of date and methodology applied across all 19 issuers. The run sandbox blocks direct access to query1.finance.yahoo.com (CONNECT tunnel refused, 403 Forbidden) — the Actions snapshot is the designed primary path for this reason. The snapshot confirmed complete = true with no per-ticker errors. No price data gaps this cycle; no incomplete banner required.

Operating detail (peer results)
% Leased, blended cash rent spread, same-property NOI growth, dividend, FFO guidance, and SP NOI guidance for all 9 direct peers are sourced from each company’s most recent quarterly earnings press release and/or investor supplemental. All figures reflect Q2 2026 (quarter ended June 30, 2026) — the most recent quarter reported as of the covered week (October 13–16, 2026). Q3 2026 earnings season begins October 27 (BRX, PECO), with all 9 direct peers scheduled to report by October 30, 2026. Figures will be refreshed in next week’s cycle. Where a peer’s supplemental was not accessible, figures marked N/D (not disclosed) or with a footnote superscript reflect either non-disclosure or best-available prior-quarter data.

Rent-step disclosure quality note
Contractual rent escalator (rent-step) disclosure is thin industry-wide among shopping center REITs. Among the 9-peer direct comp set, only KRG (~1.5–2.0% annually) and PECO consistently provide a clean contractual escalator figure in public filings. Most peers do not separately quantify step-up schedules. This is an industry-wide disclosure limitation, not a gap in this report’s research process.
