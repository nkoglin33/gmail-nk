Methodology. This report covers 19 actively-traded public retail REITs organized into 4 segments: Convenience/Strip (1 name), Grocery-Anchored/Power (9), Mall/Lifestyle (5), Net Lease (4). Names that are no longer publicly traded or have been acquired (WSR, ROIC, UBA, PREIT, STOR) are excluded and do not appear as rows, even blank ones. Segments are fixed and were not modified from the prior cycle.
Price data. Yahoo Finance chart API (primary source: https://query1.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart/{TICKER}?range=1y&interval=1d) was blocked by the environment proxy (policy denial, all 19 tickers). Prices were sourced via web search from financial news references and earnings press releases. Confirmed July 31, 2026 closes: BRX $31.50, FRT $124.09, KIM $25.51, KRG $28.94, PECO $42.44, IVT $35.20, ADC $77.77, SPG $229.37. Remaining 11 tickers (REG, CURB, AKR, MAC, CBL, SKT, NNN, EPRT, BNL, GTY, UE) are estimated from nearby-date references and analyst commentary and are labeled as estimates. 5-day return = July 31, 2026 vs. July 24, 2026 (5 trading sessions prior). YTD = July 31 vs. December 31, 2025 close. 1-Yr = July 31, 2026 vs. approximately July 31, 2025.
