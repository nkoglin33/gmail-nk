Methodology. This report covers 19 actively-traded public retail REITs organized into 4 segments: Convenience/Strip (1 name), Grocery-Anchored/Power (9), Mall/Lifestyle (5), Net Lease (4). Names that are no longer publicly traded or have been acquired (WSR, ROIC, UBA, PREIT, STOR, INRE) are excluded and do not appear as rows, even blank ones. Segments are fixed and were not modified from the prior cycle. This is a live web research pull — not a Bloomberg/FactSet-certified data extract — and should be spot-checked against certified sources before board-level or investment-committee use. Operating detail for all 9 direct REG peers is sourced from their most recent quarterly earnings releases; where a new release was not available this cycle, the prior figure is carried forward and labeled by quarter.

This is the third consecutive cloud-run cycle with this failure. All 19 tickers were attempted; all returned the same proxy block. Per standing protocol, prices and return metrics in the heatmap are carried forward from the last confirmed committed closes: Friday, August 7, 2026. 5D = Aug 7 close vs. July 31 close; YTD = Aug 7 close vs. December 31, 2025 close; 1-Yr = Aug 7 close vs. August 7, 2025 close. All price/return cells are STALE — they reflect the week ending August 7, 2026, not the week ending September 4, 2026 (the covered week). No web-search return estimates, secondary aggregators, or briefings-folder files were used as substitutes (per protocol). The INCOMPLETE BANNER at the top of the report explicitly flags this condition. The price-stale issue is a cloud-run environment constraint; when prices are next available via direct API pull from a workstation, the heatmap can be corrected and recommitted.
