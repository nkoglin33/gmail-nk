REG's 240 bps leased-to-commenced gap (~96.9% leased vs. ~94.5% paying) is the single largest source of identified future NOI among the comp set.

VI. Sector Themes — Q2 2026. (a) Sub-1% new supply in grocery-anchored nationally continues to set the structural floor under rent growth. (b) Private-market transaction volume for grocery-anchored assets reached ~$12.8B trailing-twelve-months, with institutional buyers at a decade-high participation rate and clearing prices at 5.25–5.5% cap rates — ~55 bps inside the ~6.0% implied public cap for the grocery-anchored peer set, creating a private-market value bridge that supports further multiple expansion. (d) Macerich's -6.8% selloff this week on soft SP NOI growth and 7.9× leverage illustrates the widening quality gap between necessity-anchored open-air (BRX 5.6× leverage, PECO 5.1×) and overleveraged discretionary-format operators — a gap that is pricing into the market in real time.
